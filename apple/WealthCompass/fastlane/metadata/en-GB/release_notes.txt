Wealth Compass now runs on iPad.

iPad
• The full desktop layout comes to iPad — sidebar, multi-column dashboard and the same menus as the Mac app, reflowing when you hold the device upright.
• Charts, tables and menus are built for touch, and the app icon is drawn at iPad sizes.
• Tap a chart to inspect a value; scrolling keeps working.

In your language
• The PDF report is now translated into every language the app supports, not just English.
• Dates, numbers and percentages follow the language set in the app.
• Category, sector, geography and currency names are translated.
• Many translation fixes: the tab bar no longer shows English for everyone, "NET" is no longer translated as "network", and the 3M/6M/12M ranges are no longer read as millions.

Privacy Mode conceals more
• Allocation charts are hidden, not only the figures they carry.
• Counts, status cards, empty states and alerts no longer reveal what you have recorded.
• Recurring reminders no longer name the schedule on the Lock Screen.
• The app-switcher snapshot now also covers open sheets.

Amounts are exact
• Imported and saved amounts keep full decimal precision, so figures no longer drift.
• An imported "1,000" is read as one thousand, and Indian lakh grouping is understood.
• Re-importing a Revolut statement no longer wipes your crypto edits.
• Imports no longer lose a transaction's currency, and new investments keep the currency you picked instead of defaulting to EUR.

Reports
• Cash-flow sections stay inside the period they state, the Money Flow diagram adds up to the tables, and the trend chart no longer drops months.
• Offline exports disclose the exchange rates used to convert.
• Long amounts are no longer truncated.

Also in this release
• Recurring schedules finish instead of vanishing, stay paused through unrelated edits, and can be deleted once they are done.
• More reliable iCloud sync, including in the background.
• Erase Everything no longer leaves data that comes back.
• VoiceOver labels on chart and filter controls.
