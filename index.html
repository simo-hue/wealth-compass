Dashboard

The whole picture, on one screen

Net worth, what it is made of, and how it got there. Cash, investments, crypto and debts are added up together, in a single currency, with the history behind the number.

Cash flow

Where the money goes

Income and expenses by month, grouped by category. Mark a transaction as recurring and it books itself on schedule, with a local reminder if you want one.

Investments

Positions, cost basis, profit and loss

Stocks and ETFs priced from Finnhub using an API key you provide. See allocation by sector, by type and by geography, and what each position has actually done.

Crypto

Coins, valued alongside everything else

Holdings priced from CoinGecko and converted into your currency, so crypto sits in the same total as your current account rather than in a separate app.

Settings

Your rules

Change the display currency, hide every figure with Privacy Mode, or switch language — the app speaks 35. The controls on this screen are live: change one and every screen on this page changes with it.

Also on Mac · same purchase

On the Mac, it spreads out

The same data, in a window with room for it. Six metrics across, three allocation rings side by side, and a table you can actually read. Buy it once and it is on both.
